## 3. Collaborator guide and recovery

- **Depends on:** dashboard/1 and dashboard/2
- **Contract:**
  - **In:** Implemented film-start and work-package views.
  - **Requires:** A new collaborator and a film paused at a representative
    workflow stage.
  - **Delivers:** In-product onboarding/help and a tested resume/handoff
    procedure covering permissions, current state, pending approvals, source
    links, and recovery from incomplete work.
- **Pre-work:** Dashboard views available for usability review.
- **Out of scope:** Replacing the production workflow documentation or
  automatically granting collaborator access.
- **Acceptance checks:**
  - A new user can explain the workflow stages and how to resume without
    relying on verbal guidance from the original creator.
  - The guide explains the roles of the readiness, decision-facilitator, and
    specialist agents.
  - It distinguishes exploratory proposals from approved decisions and
    explains how to handle stale or conflicting state.
  - It explains the difference between a narrative memo, a structured
    production specification (for example, a shot list or prop register),
    and an implementation asset (for example, a scene, simulation, or mocap
    file); it also explains who may author and approve each.
- **Tests:** Observed onboarding and handoff with a user not involved in
  implementation.
